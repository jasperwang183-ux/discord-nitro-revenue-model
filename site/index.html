    How the model works

    1. Sign-ups: monthly active users × a conversion rate by region × plan mix (Nitro / Basic, monthly / annual). Some start on a free trial.
    2. Cohorts: each month's sign-ups shrink along a survival curve. Monthly plans lose most people in months 1–3; annual plans keep everyone for a year, then renew or leave.
    3. Reactivation: a share of recent churners come back each month on a monthly plan.
    4. Revenue: paying subscribers × net price: list price, regional pricing, discounts, VAT, and app store fees (Apple 30% in a subscriber's first year, 15% after).

    Every input is sourced and dated in the repo. Discord is private, so every Discord figure here is a third-party estimate. This is an independent portfolio project, not affiliated with Discord, and is about method rather than precision.
